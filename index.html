Space Health · Hackathon

ASTROCARE AI

Problem
Astronaut crews need early, explainable warnings about health trends during long missions.
What I built

Expert System

AI-Based Smart Career Counseling

Problem
Students pick careers without personalised, explainable guidance on the skills they are missing.
What I built
A rule-based expert system with a knowledge base, inference engine and forward chaining. It gives explainable recommendations, skill-gap analysis and personalised roadmaps across 10 career domains.
What I learned
Designing a knowledge base and inference rules so every recommendation can be explained.

AutoML

AutoML for Small Businesses

Problem
Small businesses can't afford a data scientist to clean data and compare models.
What I built
A pipeline that automates missing-value imputation, encoding and feature scaling, then compares five-plus classifiers with clear metrics and visualisations — cutting experimentation time by about 60%.
What I learned
Building reusable preprocessing pipelines and choosing fair metrics to compare models.
PythonScikit-LearnPandas

Computer Vision

Face Detection Attendance System

Problem
Manual attendance is slow and easy to fake in classrooms.
What I built
Real-time OpenCV attendance at 30+ FPS, with lighting normalisation and timestamped SQL records — reducing attendance-logging time by about 80%.
What I learned
Optimising a real-time vision loop and handling poor lighting in real rooms.
